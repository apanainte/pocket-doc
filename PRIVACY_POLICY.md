# Privacy Policy for Pocket Doc

**Last updated:** July 10, 2024

## Introduction

Pocket Doc ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Documents**: Photos and PDF files you upload to the app
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **Usage Data**: App features you use, search queries

### How We Collect Information
- **Direct Upload**: When you upload documents through the app
- **Camera Access**: When you take photos of documents
- **Photo Library**: When you select images from your device
- **App Usage**: When you interact with app features

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **Document Management**: Store and organize your uploaded documents
- **AI Analysis**: Analyze document content to generate metadata and tags
- **Search Functionality**: Enable you to search through your documents
- **App Functionality**: Provide core app features and improvements

### AI Processing
- **Local Processing**: All AI analysis is performed locally on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: Your documents are never uploaded to external servers
- **Privacy-First**: We prioritize keeping your data on your device

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **Device-Only**: All documents are stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not sync your data to external servers
- **Secure Storage**: Documents are stored using iOS/Android secure storage

### Security Measures
- **Biometric Protection**: Optional Face ID/Touch ID authentication
- **Local Encryption**: Documents are encrypted on your device
- **No Network Transmission**: Your documents never leave your device

## Information Sharing

### We Do Not Share
- **Your Documents**: Never shared with third parties
- **Personal Data**: Never sold or shared for advertising
- **Usage Analytics**: No tracking or analytics data shared

### Limited Sharing
- **App Store**: Basic app usage statistics (anonymous)
- **Crash Reports**: Anonymous crash data for app improvement

## Your Rights

### Access and Control
- **View Your Data**: All your documents are accessible in the app
- **Delete Data**: Remove documents at any time
- **Export Data**: Download your documents from the app

### Privacy Controls
- **Camera Permissions**: Control camera access in device settings
- **Photo Library**: Control photo library access in device settings
- **Biometric**: Enable/disable biometric authentication

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app.

## Legal Basis

- **CCPA**: California Consumer Privacy Act
